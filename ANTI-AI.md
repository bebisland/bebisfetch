# 🛑 Anti-AI Policy

This is a human project.  

This project strictly prohibits the use of AI/LLM tools in all aspects of its development and maintenance.

If you used AI to generate or "improve", you’re not contributing, you’re polluting.

Vibe coding is intellectual laziness dressed up as productivity. Copy-pasting code you don’t understand and hoping the maintainers clean up after you is not contribution, it’s freeloading.

All contributions must be written by humans. Suspected AI-generated contributions will be rewritten by a human or closed without discussion.

**We can tell.** And we will not waste time arguing with people who outsource their thinking.

Write it yourself or stay out.
